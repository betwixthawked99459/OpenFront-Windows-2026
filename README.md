## 🕹️ About

OpenFront is a massively multiplayer real-time strategy game set on shared maps inspired by real-world geography and abstract battlefields. Players expand territory, manage resources, build defenses and infrastructure, form alliances, and fight for domination against other players and bots. The Windows release is planned for Early Access on September 17, 2026.

OpenFront is for players who enjoy fast-paced grand strategy, territorial competition, diplomacy, and constantly shifting multiplayer battles.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Shared World Maps** — Compete across large maps with many nations fighting simultaneously.
- ⚔️ **Real-Time Conquest** — Expand into neutral territory and attack rival nations in real time.
- 🤝 **Alliances and Diplomacy** — Form strategic alliances, negotiate with rivals, and adapt when partnerships collapse.
- 🏗️ **Economic Development** — Manage resources and build cities, ports, and defensive structures.
- 🌊 **Naval Warfare** — Control waterways, trade routes, ports, and naval engagements.
- ☢️ **Nuclear Escalation** — Use nuclear weapons to disrupt powerful opponents and reshape the battlefield.
- 🤖 **Player and Bot Battles** — Play against other players, bots, or mixed matches across supported modes.
